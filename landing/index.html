Board

Every cable matters

Drag cables between real jacks, run two amps in parallel, and patch delay or reverb into an amp’s effects loop. Plug your pedals into power to bring them to life.

30 pedals · 9 amps · 4 cabs

Grid

Read the whole signal chain

See the chain in signal order and jump to any block. Drop in a Neural Amp Modeler capture alongside your pedals or browse captures and impulse responses from TONE3000.

Mixer

Find the right balance

Balance your rig on channel strips. In the cab studio, drag up to four mics across the speaker, or blend two impulse responses to shape the cabinet sound.

Live

Keep the set moving

Organize your sounds into songs and scenes. Keep the current sound, the next cue, your tuner and tap tempo in sight while you play.

Plug in and play

Open Rigsmith on your Mac, connect your guitar through an audio interface, and start with a preset or build a rig from scratch.
