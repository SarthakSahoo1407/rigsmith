Read the whole signal chain

See the chain in signal order and jump to any block. Drop in a Neural Amp Modeler capture alongside your pedals or browse captures and impulse responses from TONE3000.

  • Signal order

    Jump to any block in the chain.

Mixer

Find the right balance

Balance your rig on channel strips. In the cab studio, drag up to four mics across the speaker, or blend two impulse responses to shape the cabinet sound.

  • Up to four mics

    Drag them across the speaker in the cab studio.

  • Two impulse responses

    Blend two to shape the cabinet sound.
